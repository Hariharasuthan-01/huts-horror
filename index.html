HOW TO PLAY

1. One player creates a team, the other joins with the code.
2. Player 1 is BLUE, Player 2 is RED - you can only take your own key.
3. One player may view the map ONCE for 10 seconds.
4. Memorize it and talk to your teammate.
5. Explore the house with your flashlight.
6. Avoid the creature: crouch and walk to be quiet, running is loud.
7. Find your key. Find the secret gun if you can: 6 rounds + 6 reserve, it only stuns the creature.
8. At 0 lives a player is down; a teammate with 2+ lives can GIVE 1 LIFE (G).
9. When both keys are found, find the exit. BOTH players must reach it.
Keys: WASD move, mouse look, SHIFT sprint, C/CTRL crouch, E interact, F flashlight, R reload, CLICK shoot, G give life, ESC pause.
